Fix IPTV audio out of sync on Smart TV: guide for 2026

✍️ David Shaw · 📅 October 2026 · ⏱️ 5 min read · 🛡️ Verified Review

If your IPTV audio is out of sync on your Smart TV, the problem is usually a small setting or network issue. This guide walks you through the fastest fixes first, then the harder ones.

Each fix is written in simple steps you can follow right now.

What causes IPTV audio sync problems on Smart TV?

Audio out of sync means the sound arrives earlier or later than the picture. Common causes include:

  • Buffering or internet speed drops
  • Wrong audio output setting on the TV or player app
  • App or firmware not updated
  • Network interference from your ISP

Most fixes take less than two minutes. Try them in the order below.

Quick checks (30 seconds each)

Cause: Internet speed is too low → Fix: Run a speed test

IPTV in 4K needs at least 25 Mbps. Use Speedtest on your TV or phone while streaming. If your speed is below 25 Mbps, restart your router or move closer to the Wi‑Fi source.

Cause: Wi‑Fi interference → Fix: Switch to wired connection

Wireless signals can cause delays. Connect your Smart TV directly to the router with an Ethernet cable. This gives a stable connection that stops audio drift.

Settings fixes on your Smart TV (2–5 minutes)

Cause: Audio output format mismatch → Fix: Change audio settings

Many Smart TVs output “auto” audio, which can confuse IPTV apps. Try this:

  1. Go to Settings → Sound → Audio Output (exact names vary by TV brand).
  2. Set to PCM or Stereo instead of Dolby Digital or Auto.
  3. Test the IPTV stream again.

If that doesn’t help, turn off any “lip sync” or “audio delay” option first, then add a manual delay of +50 ms until sync improves.

Cause: Refresh rate mismatch → Fix: Match TV refresh rate to stream

Some Smart TVs run at 60 Hz, but IPTV streams may be 50 Hz or 24 fps. Go to Settings → Picture → Expert Settings → Motion Settings and turn off any “smooth motion” or “truMotion” feature. Then set the refresh rate to match the stream (usually 60 Hz for most IPTV).

Player app fixes (the most common cause)

SubTV IP works best on tested players: TiviMate 5.x, IBO Player Pro, IPTV Smarters Pro, Kodi 21, and Flex IPTV. Below are specific sync fixes for the two most popular players.

Cause: TiviMate buffer or sync setting → Fix: Adjust buffer and audio offset

In TiviMate 5.x:

  1. Open the app and go to Settings → Playback.
  2. Set Buffer size to 5,000 ms (this matches SubTV IP’s recommended cache setting).
  3. If audio is still off, go to Audio → Audio offset and use the remote to adjust – press + or – until lip movements match sound.
  4. Save and restart the stream.

Cause: IPTV Smarters Pro sync setting → Fix: Use external player

  1. In IPTV Smarters Pro, go to Settings → Playback → Player.
  2. Switch from “Built-in” to VLC or MX Player (install them first if missing).
  3. In VLC, pause the stream then press the audio sync button (or go to Audio → Audio delay) and adjust by 0.1 seconds at a time.
  4. Resume and check.

For Kodi 21, open Settings → Player → Audio and enable “Sync playback to display”. Then adjust audio offset in the same menu.

Network and ISP fixes (takes 3–5 minutes)

Some internet providers slow down video streaming. SubTV IP uses anti‑freeze server clusters with 99.9% uptime, but your ISP can still cause audio lag.

Cause: ISP throttling → Fix: Use Cloudflare DNS and larger buffer

Two changes help:

  1. Change DNS on your Smart TV or router to Cloudflare 1.1.1.1. On most Smart TVs: Settings → Network → IP Settings → DNS → Manual → enter 1.1.1.1 and 1.0.0.1.
  2. Increase buffer size to 5,000 ms in your IPTV app (as shown in the TiviMate fix above).

This combination stops packet loss that causes audio to drift.

What to do if nothing worked

If you tried all fixes above and audio is still out of sync:

  • Update your Smart TV firmware to the latest version (check under Settings → Support → Software Update).
  • Update your IPTV player app to the newest version from the app store.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my IPTV audio out of sync only on some channels?

Different broadcasters use different audio codecs. The channel may be sending a 50 Hz stream while your TV runs at 60 Hz. Use the audio offset adjustment in your player to fix it per channel.

Does turning off Dolby Digital fix audio sync on Smart TV?

Yes, often. Many Smart TVs add extra processing time for Dolby Digital. Setting audio output to PCM or Stereo removes that delay.

Can a slow internet connection cause IPTV audio delay?

Yes. When the internet is slow, the video buffers but audio might play ahead. Run a Speedtest. Speeds below 25 Mbps for 4K streams often cause sync issues.

Will changing DNS really fix audio out of sync?

Changing DNS to Cloudflare 1.1.1.1 can reduce packet loss and jitter, which are common causes of audio drift. It’s a simple step that costs nothing.

How do I fix audio sync on TiviMate 5.x?

Go to Settings → Playback and set Buffer size to 5,000 ms. Then go to Audio → Audio offset and adjust with the remote until sound matches picture.

What is the best buffer size for IPTV audio sync on a Smart TV?

SubTV IP recommends 5,000 ms (5 seconds). This gives enough time to smooth out network fluctuations without creating noticeable delay.
